Account Deletion Request

To delete your account in our application, please use one of the following methods:

📧 Via Email

Send an email to auraniaapp@outlook.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" using your registered email address.

📌 Process

  • Your request will be processed within 72 hours
  • All your personal data will be permanently deleted

📂 Deleted Data

  • User account
  • All data associated with the user
